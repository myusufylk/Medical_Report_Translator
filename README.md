# 🩺 Welcome to Medical Translator!

We translate your medical reports into simple, everyday language everyone can understand.

## 📖 How It Works?

1.  **Select Report Type:** Choose the type of report you want to translate (Blood Test, Epikris, etc.).
2.  **Upload Document:** Take a clear photo of your report or upload it from your gallery.
3.  **Review Analysis:** Read the simplified results of technical terms and medical findings in plain English.

## 🚀 Why Medical Translator?

*   **Clarity:** Translates complex medical jargon into "layman's terms."
*   **Focus:** Highlights only findings that need your attention, ignoring irrelevant normal values.
*   **Speed:** Powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash for comprehensive results in seconds.

---

> ⚠️ **Important Note:** This application is for informational purposes only. Results do not constitute medical diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a **healthcare professional** for definitive interpretation and treatment.

# 🩺 Medikal Tercüman'a Hoş Geldiniz!

Tıbbi raporlarınızı herkesin anlayabileceği sade bir dile çeviriyoruz.

1.  **Rapor Türünü Seçin:** Kan tahlili, EKG veya Epikriz seçeneklerinden size uygun olanı belirleyin.
